# Retail module

The retail API is driven by `database/kiot_retail_full_postgresql.sql`.

- All 156 database tables are registered as TypeORM entities and exposed through the retail CRUD module.
- Fifteen tables with business-specific columns use dedicated entities; the remaining schema tables use generated entities matching their shared record shape.
- Routes are mounted below `/v1/retail`; `GET /v1/retail/resources` lists every available resource.
- The resource metadata response includes every table column, writable fields, required fields, PostgreSQL/TypeORM types, enum values, generated/primary/nullable flags, and relation targets so the frontend can build complete typed CRUD forms for all 156 resources.
- Generic `reference_id` links are declared per resource in the shared table definition (for example `return_items -> returns`, `invoice_items -> invoices`, `voucher_transactions -> vouchers`, and `payroll_items -> payrolls`); generated CRUD validates the referenced row and tenant before insert/update.
- Every resource with a `tenant_id` requires the `x-tenant-id` header. The `tenants` resource is available without that header for bootstrap.
- `RetailService` only accepts fields declared by the table definition and discards protected fields such as `id` and timestamps.
- Tables with `deleted_at` support DELETE through TypeORM soft delete. Tables without it reject DELETE.
- Every generated module follows the reference layering: repository extends `BaseRepository<Entity>`, service extends `BaseService<Entity>`, and controller extends `BaseController<Service>`.
- Route files apply per-action permissions plus body, query, and UUID param schemas with `zodValidate`.
- `POST /v1/retail/checkout` validates branch, warehouse, customer, and variants before opening the order write; it commits order, order items, payment, inventory decrement, stock ledger entries, and customer totals in one TypeORM transaction.
- Checkout requires `branchId` and at least one `{ variantId, quantity, unitPrice }` item. `warehouseId` enables pessimistic inventory locking and stock decrement; omit it for an order that does not affect inventory.
- Checkout resolves `paymentMethodId` from the tenant payment-method catalog, validates active branch/warehouse/customer/product/variant records, rejects duplicate variants, and rejects a warehouse belonging to another branch.
- `POST /v1/retail/inventory/adjust` records stock adjustments as one transaction: it validates an active warehouse and active variants, rejects duplicate variants and negative available stock, updates `inventories`, and appends `ADJUSTMENT` rows to `stock_ledgers` linked to the adjustment record.
- `POST /v1/retail/inventory/count` records physical counted quantities, rejects counts below reserved stock, reconciles `inventories`, creates `stock_counts`/`stock_count_items`, and writes `COUNT_ADJUSTMENT` ledger rows only for actual differences.
- `POST /v1/retail/inventory/transfer` moves stock between two active tenant warehouses atomically, creating a transfer record/items plus `TRANSFER_OUT` and `TRANSFER_IN` ledger entries.
- `POST /v1/retail/purchasing/receive` receives stock into an active warehouse atomically, validates optional supplier and purchase-order links, creates receipt/items, updates `inventories`, and appends `PURCHASE_RECEIPT` ledger entries.
- `POST /v1/retail/purchasing/orders` creates a linked purchase order and purchase-order items for an active warehouse, supplier, and SKU set. Receiving against that order validates ordered quantities, tracks `receivedQuantity`, moves the order through `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED`/`RECEIVED`, and infers the supplier for debt creation when omitted from the receipt.
- A goods receipt with a supplier and positive cost also creates a linked active `supplier_debts` record and `supplier_debt_transactions` entry. The receipt and item rows store the warehouse, supplier, variant, quantity, and unit cost in `data` for later reconciliation.
- `POST /v1/retail/purchasing/return` accepts a completed goods receipt and selected receipt items, prevents returning more than received or more than available stock, creates `purchase_returns`/`purchase_return_items`, decreases inventory, appends `PURCHASE_RETURN` ledger entries, and reduces the linked supplier debt atomically.
- `POST /v1/retail/suppliers/debt/pay` validates the supplier and payment method, allocates an outbound payment across the oldest active supplier debts, writes supplier debt transactions, and creates a linked receipt.
- Completed sales payments, customer debt collections, and supplier debt payments also append linked `cash_movements` records with explicit `IN`/`OUT` direction and payment method references.
- Checkout accepts `priceBookId`, `promotionId`, and `couponCode`. Active `price_book_items` use `reference_id` or `data.priceBookId` to identify the price book and `data.variantId` plus `amount`/`data.unitPrice` for the SKU price. Coupons can point to a promotion through `data.promotionId`; promotion data supports `discountType` (`PERCENT` or `FIXED`), `value`, `minSubtotal`, `maxDiscount`, `startsAt`, and `endsAt`. Applied discounts and coupon uses are persisted in `order_discounts` and `coupon_usages` inside the checkout transaction.
- Checkout calculates tax when a line does not provide `taxTotal`: active `product_tax_rates` rows identify a variant/product and point to `tax_rates` through `reference_id` or `data.taxRateId`; the rate can be stored as `data.rate`, `data.percent`, or `amount`. Each calculated tax is persisted in `order_taxes` and included in the order total.
- A completed checkout also creates the linked `invoices`/`invoice_items`, `fulfillments`/`fulfillment_items` when a warehouse is selected, `order_status_history`, and `payment_transactions` when a payment exists. These records use the order or payment IDs in `reference_id` and repeat the link in `data`.
- `POST /v1/retail/orders/:id/ship` validates an active shipping provider when supplied, creates linked `shipping_orders`, `shipments`, and `shipment_items`, moves the fulfillment and order to `SHIPPED`, and records the order status history atomically.
- `POST /v1/retail/orders/:id/deliver` only accepts a shipped order, moves shipment, shipping order, shipment items, fulfillment, and order to `DELIVERED`, and records delivery history atomically.
- Sales returns create both the refund records and the business `returns`/`return_items` records; full returns mark the linked invoice `REFUNDED`, while partial returns mark it `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`. Cancellation marks linked invoice and fulfillment records `CANCELLED`.
- `POST /v1/retail/cash-sessions/open` and `POST /v1/retail/cash-sessions/:id/close` manage one open session per cash register, calculate expected cash from opening balance plus linked cash movements, and persist closing difference. Checkout accepts `cashSessionId` only with an open session and the `CASH` payment method.
- `POST /v1/retail/attendance/punch` locks one employee/date/shift attendance row, records check-in or check-out, and calculates worked, late, early-leave, and overtime minutes from the selected work shift.
- `POST /v1/retail/payroll/generate` creates draft payrolls and linked `payroll_items` for active employees from a date range or payroll period, using attendance days and overtime as the calculation inputs; repeated generation for the same payroll period is idempotent per employee.
- Gift cards and vouchers have transactional issue/redeem endpoints at `/gift-cards/issue`, `/gift-cards/redeem`, `/vouchers/issue`, and `/vouchers/redeem`. Each endpoint locks the balance, checks expiry/limits, updates the remaining amount, and writes the matching transaction table.
- Checkout creates or updates a `loyalty_accounts` record for a customer and awards points using `data.pointRate` (default 10,000 VND/point) and the active tier `data.multiplier`; each award is recorded in `loyalty_transactions`. Cancellation and returns reverse earned points in the same transaction.
- `POST /v1/retail/orders/:id/cancel` is the only supported completed-order cancellation path: it restores inventory, reverses paid payments through a refund record, updates customer totals, and records order status history in one transaction. Generic order CRUD rejects direct `status=CANCELLED` updates.
- Checkout accepts `paidAmount`; partial or unpaid orders require a customer, set `paymentStatus` to `PARTIAL`/`UNPAID`, and create linked customer debt and debt-transaction records.
- `POST /v1/retail/orders/:id/return` validates return quantities against the original order and previous refunds, restores inventory, creates refund/items, updates payment/order/customer/debt totals, and writes `SALE_RETURN` plus status history atomically.
- `POST /v1/retail/orders/:id/exchange` handles a real exchange transaction: it prevents reusing returned quantities, creates `returns`/`return_items` and `exchanges`/`exchange_items`, restores the returned SKUs, deducts replacement stock, creates the replacement order/invoice, and settles a positive or negative difference through payment, debt, refund, and cash movement records.
- `POST /v1/retail/customers/debt/collect` validates an active customer and payment method, allocates one payment across oldest active debts, settles debt records and transactions, and creates a receipt.
- `POST /v1/retail/financial/reconcile` compares payments with payment transactions and cash movements for a date range, then stores one reconciliation parent plus linked reconciliation items for every matched or divergent row.
- Core relation migration adds database foreign keys for store/branch, attendance/shift, payroll/payroll-period, product category/brand/unit, and payment method references. The service also validates inferred UUID relations before writes.
- Generic workflow UUIDs stored in `data` are declared in `RETAIL_DATA_RELATIONS`; `/v1/retail/resources` exposes them as `dataRelations`, and the shared `BaseRepository` validates them for generated resource routes as well as the aggregate retail route.
- Regenerate all table modules with `yarn retail:module:generate` after changing the generator or shared retail controller behavior.
